## Description

Alliance selection is a live draft. Your list is a plan; the board is
reality. This ticket is the rules of the process, the social layer (who
will accept), and how to recover when the first three names are already
taken.

You bring the one-page captain board from
[Building a Pick List](../pick-lists/).
You do not reopen the pick meeting on the carpet. You *do* have an "if
they are gone" next name for the first six picks. The scout lead or
coach stands with the captain. One voice to the announcer.

Read this season's manual section on alliance selection. For 2026 that
is **Section 10.6.1 ALLIANCE Selection Process** in the
[Game Manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf).
The shape is stable year to year: top seeds become alliance leads;
captains invite; the invited student accepts or declines immediately;
round 1 is 1→8, round 2 is 8→1; declining teams generally cannot be
picked again (see T606). Timers are real — 45 seconds in round 1, 90
seconds in round 2 in 2026 (T605). If your season's numbers differ,
the *current* manual wins.

A decline can burn a relationship and a ranking. Do not decline
lightly. Do not promise a pick you cannot make. Do not bluff a captain
in the pits on Friday. Third robots and backups matter more than
pride. A functional third beats an impressive DNP.

Watch a recorded selection as a rehearsal, not as entertainment. After
you pick, immediately start the match-strategy conversation — the next
ticket is the one-pager. Do not celebrate so long you skip auto
planning.

This site does not run your draft. Paper and a student at the mic do.

## Prerequisites

- [Building a Pick List](../pick-lists/)
  completed (captain board, justification paragraph, "if they are
  gone" names).
- Willingness to role-play out loud. Silent reading will not get the
  timer.

## What you'll learn

- How the official selection process works this season, including
  accept / decline and the clock.
- How to think about who will accept without lying in the pits.
- How to pick in 30 seconds when the plan dies.

## Tasks

1. **Read the named manual section.** Read
   **Section 10.6.1** through T606 (and T601–T605) in the
   [2026 Game Manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
   or this year's "Alliance Selection" section in
   [Season Materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials).
   Write, in your own words, without looking back:

   - who is an alliance lead
   - who may speak at the mic (captain vs invited student)
   - what happens if a team declines (can they be picked later? backup
     pool?)
   - round 1 order vs round 2 order
   - what the pick timer is this year

   If you cannot do this from memory, you are not ready to stand next
   to a captain.

2. **Watch a recorded selection.** Open a 2026 (or latest)
   championship or district championship event on
   [TBA](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)
   — Einstein's alliances are on
   [2026cmptx](https://www.thebluealliance.com/event/2026cmptx);
   division pages (Newton, Daly, and the rest) show who picked whom.
   Watch the alliance-selection video if TBA or YouTube has it for
   that event; otherwise watch any official FIRST "alliance selection"
   recording your mentor names and follow along on the TBA alliances
   tab. Write three notes: a pick that matches a "best available
   scorer" theory, a pick that looks like a role fill, and a decline
   or surprise if one happened.

3. **Script the one sentence at the mic.** Two scripts, on the captain
   board:

   - Invite: "Alliance \_\_\_\_ picks team \_\_\_\_."
   - Accept: "Team \_\_\_\_ accepts."
   - Decline: the shortest honest decline you would actually say, plus
     a written rule for *when* you would use it.

   Only a student speaks (T603). Practice once out loud. If you giggle
   through it in the shop, you will freeze on the field.

4. **Role-play a dead plan.** With two teammates, start from your
   locked list. Cross out your first two names (they got taken). The
   clock is 30 seconds — use a phone timer. Make a pick from the
   remaining list. Say the invite sentence. Then do it again: first
   *six* names gone, 30 seconds, pick. Write what you picked and which
   line on the board saved you. If the board did not have a next name,
   that is a failed artifact from the last ticket; fix the board.

5. **Social layer, Friday, not Saturday.** List three teams you would
   talk to in the pits *before* selection, and the one question you
   would ask: "If we are in a position to pick you, do you want to
   play with a robot that does X?" You already practiced pit questions
   in
   [Pit Scouting](../pit-scouting/).
   Do not promise a pick. Note teams that have already agreed with
   someone else if you hear it. Surprises on the carpet are usually
   avoidable.

6. **After the pick.** Write the first 10 minutes: who walks to the
   new partners, who starts the auto drawing, who updates the backup-
   pool watch. The
   [Pre-Match Strategy](../match-strategy/)
   one-pager starts now, not after dinner.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] This season's accept / decline rules, pick order, and timer are
      explained in your own words, checkable against Section 10.6.1
      (or this year's equivalent).
- [ ] Notes from a recorded selection exist (three observations).
- [ ] Invite / accept / decline sentences are written and have been
      said out loud.
- [ ] A role-play happened where the first two names were gone and a
      pick was made in 30 seconds from the list; a second play with
      six names gone is recorded.
- [ ] Three pre-selection pit conversations are listed with the
      question, not a promise.
- [ ] A mentor has heard the mic sentence and the 30-second pick.

## Notes

- T606 in 2026: a captain may not invite a team that already declined
  another alliance; declining teams are ineligible to be a backup.
  Confirm this year's rule before you treat a decline as "maybe
  later."
- Alliance leads who decline can still captain; they cannot be
  invited. Orange-highlighted "will become captain" teams are a
  special case on the audience display — read the manual, do not
  invent the color code.
- Backup teams exist (Section 10.6.3). Pride is not a lineup.

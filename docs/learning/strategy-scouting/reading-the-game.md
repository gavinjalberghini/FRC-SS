## Description

Highlight videos lie in a useful way. They show what is possible at the
highest level of play, not what is legal, common, or worth points for *your*
team in week 6. Strategy starts with the current season's Game Manual, the
official Q&A, and the field drawings. This ticket is how to read those
without drowning — and how to write notes a designer can use on Monday.

The 2026 game is **REBUILT**. FIRST publishes the manual as a PDF and as
HTML, plus a Q&A that can legalize or kill a strategy after Kickoff. If you
are working a later season, use that year's equivalent documents from
[Season Materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials)
or the
[archived games library](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/archived-games).
The *method* does not change: scoring table, match play, robot rules,
violation guide, then field geometry.

You do not need to memorize every inspection appendix on day one. You do
need the scoring table cold, and you need a one-page brief that travels to
CAD, mechanical, and programming. Strategy that stays in the strategy
channel is a hobby.

This site does not track whether you finished and does not store your game
notes. Keep the brief in a notebook, a shared drive, or a repo your team
owns. If your team exported these tickets into its own GitHub, close this
issue there once a mentor accepts the criteria below.

The next ticket,
[Scoring, Cycles & Penalties](../scoring-and-cycles/),
turns the scoring table you copy here into cycle times and
points-per-second. Do not skip the brief; you will reuse it for the rest of
this track.

## Prerequisites

- None. This is the starting point for every scout.
- A web browser and a way to write (paper or a doc). You do not need a
  scouting app yet.

## What you'll learn

- Which sections of the Game Manual actually drive strategy, and which you
  can leave for inspection week.
- How the official Q&A changes what you thought you knew, and why "I saw a
  video" is not a citation.
- How to write a one-page game brief a CAD or mechanical lead can use
  without sitting through Kickoff with you.

## Tasks

1. **Find this season's sources.** Open
   [FRC Season Materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials)
   and locate three things: the Game Manual, the Q&A, and the playing-field
   drawings. For 2026 those are the
   [REBUILT Game Manual (PDF)](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf),
   the
   [HTML manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/HTML/2026GameManual.htm),
   [FRC Q&A](https://frc-qa.firstinspires.org/),
   and
   [Playing Field](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/playing-field).
   Bookmark all four. Write the URLs at the top of your notes so you stop
   googling "frc manual" from a random blog.

2. **Read the named scoring section.** In the 2026 manual, read
   **Section 6.5 Scoring** through **Table 6-4** (point values) and
   **Table 6-5** (bonus ranking-point thresholds). Also skim
   **Section 6.4 MATCH Periods** so you know AUTO, TELEOP, the alliance
   shifts, and END GAME. If you are on a later game, read that year's
   scoring table and match-period section — they are always near "Game
   Details." Copy the scoring table into your notes *in your own words*
   (or a clean copy with annotations). Mark, for each row: when the points
   are awarded, whether they are match points or ranking points, and what
   would make the score not count.

3. **Read the arena and robot-rule slices that kill cool ideas.** Skim
   **Section 5 ARENA** (zones, hub, bump, trench, depot, tower) and
   **Section 8 ROBOT Construction Rules** far enough to list three rules
   that would make a "cool" robot illegal — size, starting configuration,
   a mechanism FIRST already banned, a bumper rule, a height limit. Field
   drawings decide reach and human-player geometry; CAD people need those
   numbers early. Write the three rules with the rule number (G-rule or
   R-rule), not "I think you can't do that."

4. **Treat Q&A as part of the manual.** Open
   [FRC Q&A](https://frc-qa.firstinspires.org/)
   (or
   [the one-page archive](https://frc-qa.firstinspires.org/onepage.html)
   if the season is closed). Find **one** answered question that changes
   how you would play or build — a scoring clarification, a contact rule,
   a human-player limit. Paste the question number and a two-sentence
   summary into your notes. If two smart people on your team disagree
   about a rule later this season, the move is look it up, not argue from
   a highlight reel.

5. **Watch one match with the manual open.** Watch
   [2026 Einstein Finals 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EjF9we707DA)
   (Daly 4065 / 4414 / 1323 vs Newton 868 / 2910 / 2046). Keep
   [the TBA score breakdown](https://www.thebluealliance.com/match/2026cmptx_f1m1)
   next to the video. Do **not** try to count every fuel. Write five
   observations that are *rule-shaped*: which hub was active when, who
   went for the tower, a foul or near-foul, a zone constraint you can
   point at in Section 5 or 7. This is the shared match for the rest of
   the track. You will score robots from it later; today you are proving
   you can watch with the manual, not with your thumbs.

6. **Write the one-page brief.** One page, not three. It must include:

   - How a match is won, in one paragraph (not "score more points").
   - The three actions that look high-value on paper, with the point
     values from Task 2 next to them.
   - Three open questions for Q&A or for a prototype.
   - The three illegal-cool-robot rules from Task 3.

   Hand that brief to a CAD, mechanical, or programming lead — or to a
   mentor standing in for one. Strategy that nobody else can read is
   not strategy.

7. **Skim one Kickoff strategy thread.** Read enough of
   [Kickoff Day 1 Recap (3128, Chief Delphi)](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/kickoff-day-1-recap/510906)
   or
   [Rebuilt Might be Rock-Paper-Scissors](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/rebuilt-might-be-rock-paper-scissors/511558)
   to see how other teams argue from the manual. Star one claim you
   *agree* with and one you want to check against Section 6.5. Chief
   Delphi is a conversation, not a citation. The manual wins.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] You can open this season's manual, Q&A, and field drawings from
      FIRST without searching Slack for a link.
- [ ] Written game notes exist: the scoring table in your own words (or
      an annotated copy), plus when each row is awarded.
- [ ] Three rules that would make a "cool" robot illegal are listed with
      real rule numbers.
- [ ] One Q&A answer is summarized in two sentences with its question
      number.
- [ ] Five rule-shaped observations from Einstein Finals 1 (or a mentor-
      approved substitute match) are in the notes.
- [ ] A one-page brief exists with the how-we-win paragraph, three
      high-value actions, three open questions, and the illegal-robot
      list, and a mentor or another trade's lead has seen it.

## Notes

- Team Updates rewrite the manual during the season. If your printed
  copy is from Kickoff Saturday and it is now March, you are arguing
  from a draft. Check the latest PDF.
- The Q&A does not supersede the manual, but it is how FIRST tells you
  what a sentence meant. Referees still have the last word at the
  event.
- Do not memorize Section 9 (inspection) on Kickoff night. Do memorize
  where points come from.
- This website never becomes your scouting database. The brief lives
  where your team already keeps files.
- Next:
  [Scoring, Cycles & Penalties](../scoring-and-cycles/).
  Bring the scoring table. You will time cycles on video.

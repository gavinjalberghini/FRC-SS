## Description

The pick list you printed Friday is a hypothesis. Qualification matches
are the experiment. This capstone is the loop: debrief, update data,
change the plan, and tell the people who need to know — without
relitigating the season strategy in the pit.

You now have the whole chain: game notes, a cycle table, a priority
list, a scouting schema, pit and match sheets, qualitative sentences,
TBA/Statbotics sitting *next to* your cards, a defended pick list, a
draft plan, and a one-page match plan. This ticket is what you do
*between* matches so that chain stays honest.

After each of *your* matches, before the next queue: what the plan was
vs. what happened (auto, cycles, fouls, endgame); one robot or one
rule that surprised you; one change for the next match (or "run it
again"). No speeches. The drive coach and scout lead talk; everyone
else can add one sentence. That is a five-minute meeting. See
[Effective Meetings]({{ '/learning/leadership/effective-meetings/' | relative_url }})
if your debriefs keep becoming twenty.

Update the board from **rows**, not from a feeling that "they're so
much better now." Move teams on the pick list when *n* grows or a
mechanism appears or disappears. Publish a short watch list to the
pit. If the list never moves, you are not scouting — you are
decorating.

Saturday night: freeze the selection board, sleep, eat. After
selection: new one-pagers for elim partners, new auto drawings. After
the event: export the data and three lessons for the next event. Do
not wait for the offseason retrospective. This website still does not
store that export. Your team does.

## Prerequisites

- All prior Strategy & Scouting lessons (tickets 1–11).
- A filled one-pager from
  [Pre-Match Strategy](../match-strategy/)
  and a captain board from
  [Building a Pick List](../pick-lists/).

## What you'll learn

- How to run a five-minute debrief that produces one change.
- When to change a ranking or a match role, and how to show the rows
  that forced it.
- How to keep scouting staffed through Sunday without burning the same
  three people.

## Tasks

1. **Run a five-minute debrief on the shared match.** Use the Newton
   one-pager you wrote for
   [Einstein Finals 1](https://www.thebluealliance.com/match/2026cmptx_f1m1)
   (or your mentor-approved match). Timer on. Three bullets only:

   - Plan vs. reality: auto, cycles, fouls, endgame.
   - One robot or one rule that surprised you (cite
     [Section 6 or 7](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/HTML/2026GameManual.htm)
     if it is a rule).
   - One change for the next match, or the words "run it again."

   Coach and scout lead speak; others get one sentence. If you exceed
   five minutes, the debrief failed. Write the three bullets down —
   that is the artifact.

2. **Show one ranking change with the rows attached.** Take your pick
   list. Move **one** team at least two places, or swap a first-pick
   and a second-pick, *because of new data*. Attach the evidence: a
   new match sheet, a TBA replay
   ([Finals 2](https://www.thebluealliance.com/match/2026cmptx_f1m2)
   or
   [Finals 3](https://www.thebluealliance.com/match/2026cmptx_f1m3)
   if you are staying on Einstein), a disable flag, a qualitative note
   with a match key. "I changed my mind" is not evidence. If the
   sample still cannot justify a move, write that — honesty is a
   legal outcome — and instead change a *match role* on the one-pager
   with the same standard of evidence.

3. **Hand a next-match one-pager to the drive team.** Duplicate
   yesterday's page. Change at least one explicit line (auto
   assignment, defense target, endgame clock, foul watch). Give it to
   a driver. Same 20-second restatement test as the last ticket. The
   change must be visible without a lecture.

4. **Update public-data complements, do not replace the board.** Open
   [Statbotics](https://www.statbotics.io/)
   and
   [TBA](https://www.thebluealliance.com/)
   for one team you moved. Write two sentences: what the public
   number did after the extra match, and whether that agrees with your
   move. If EPA still loves a robot you just dropped, you owe the
   meeting the jammed-intake sentence — not a quiet delete.

5. **Staffing through Sunday.** Rewrite the Friday staffing plan from
   [Designing a Scouting System](../scouting-system-design/)
   for a playoff Saturday/Sunday: fewer matches, higher stakes, tired
   people, alliance-strategy meetings stealing your best scouts. Name
   who scouts elims (you still need eyes on *opponents*, not only on
   yourselves) and who is explicitly off-duty to eat. If scouting dies
   after alliance selection, your elim one-pagers will be fiction.

6. **Write three event-level lessons before the trailer is unpacked.**
   Not "we should scout more." Three things you would change at the
   *next* event: a field on the schema, a debrief habit, a pick-
   meeting time-box, a pit question, a cycle definition that broke. Put
   them in the same folder as the exported sheets. Point at
   [Season Roadmap — Competition Season]({{ '/roadmap/' | relative_url }})
   so the lessons land in the next event's prep, not in a forgotten
   slide.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A five-minute debrief was run and produced three written
      bullets (plan vs. reality, surprise, change-or-repeat).
- [ ] One ranking change *or* one match-role change exists with the
      rows or match keys attached.
- [ ] A next-match one-pager was handed to a driver with at least one
      explicit change, and they restated it.
- [ ] Two sentences exist on whether TBA/Statbotics agreed with the
      move.
- [ ] A playoff-weekend staffing note exists (who still scouts
      opponents, who is off).
- [ ] Three event-level lessons are written before "we'll do a retro
      later."
- [ ] A mentor has seen the debrief bullets, the change-with-evidence,
      and the three lessons.

## Notes

- Sleep is a strategy input. A frozen Saturday-night board plus eight
  hours beats a 2 a.m. rerank that nobody remembers.
- Do not relitigate must / should / won't in the pit between
  qualification matches. That list was a January decision. This ticket
  changes *picks* and *match roles*.
- Export the data before someone "cleans up" the shared drive. Future
  you at event two is a teammate.
- This is the last lesson on the Strategy & Scouting track. The
  artifacts — game notes, cycle table, priority list, schema, ranked
  list with a paragraph — are how a mentor knows you can run the next
  event, not a progress bar on this site.

## Description

The team’s public voice is part of the robot. A careless post can burn
a sponsor, a student, or a school administrator in an afternoon. This
ticket is the standard for what gets published, who publishes it, and
what never leaves the private chat.

You already have a [pitch, paragraph, and brand rules](../team-story-brand/)
and you have practiced saying them in
[Judge Interviews & Pit Talks](../judge-interviews/). Communications is
the same story on a slower channel: site, one social account, a fact
sheet a reporter can copy. It is not a new personality for the team.

One voice, many hands:

- A small set of official channels (site, one social, email). Personal
  accounts can cheer; they should not negotiate or announce.
- A student comms owner and an adult who can take something down.
- The brand rules from Lesson 2 apply to posts. A new filter is not a
  new identity. FIRST marks still follow
  [FIRST Brand & Assets](https://www.firstinspires.org/about/brand)
  and the
  [Branding & Design Guidelines](https://info.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/about/policy/first-brand-guidelines.pdf).

What never gets posted:

- Student last names, locations of homes, or travel plans that make a
  group findable in real time.
- Photos of minors without the consent process the school and FIRST
  require. Start at
  [Youth Protection](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/youth-protection-program).
- Internal conflict, other teams’ failures, or unreleased strategy.
- Sponsor logos in a context they did not approve (memes, political
  captions, complaints). You promised a benefit in
  [Sponsorship Fundamentals](../sponsorship-fundamentals/). A meme is
  not that benefit.

When in doubt, do not post. Ask. The story will still be there
tomorrow. The screenshot will still be there tomorrow too, which is
why “we deleted it” is not a plan.

Press and school:

- A one-page fact sheet (name, number, season goal, student contact
  that is team-owned, mentor contact) so nobody invents a quote.
- Students may speak to their own work. Policy, safety incidents, and
  official results go through the adult owner.
- Credit FIRST, the school, and partners accurately. Official
  leave-behinds live on
  [FIRST Brand and Marketing Downloads](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/brand-marketing-downloads)
  if you need a flyer you did not design.

Look at how public teams keep a consistent voice:
[Team 254](https://www.team254.com/),
[Citrus Circuits](https://citruscircuits.org/),
[Mechanical Advantage](https://www.littletonrobotics.org/).
You are noticing consistency, not copying their captions.

This playbook’s [Team Contract](../../../contract/) technology section
is local policy. FIRST’s rules and the school’s rules sit on top of
it. The stricter one wins.

**This website does not store the content calendar, the fact sheet, or
photo consent forms.** Those live in the team’s drive. Mentors review
links. This site also does not track whether you posted.

The last ticket, [Year-Round Business Leadership](../year-round-leadership/),
is the calendar that keeps this voice alive after the last event.

## Prerequisites

- [Team Story & Brand](../team-story-brand/) completed (pitch, paragraph,
  colors, voice).
- The [Team Contract](../../../contract/) technology section, read
  once.
- Access to the official channels (or a mentor who can show them) and
  the team drive.

## What you'll learn

- How to plan a simple content calendar with a purpose for each post.
- Photo, name, and location rules that protect students and sponsors.
- How to talk to press without freelancing policy.

## Tasks

1. **Read the rules you do not get to invent.** Skim
   [FIRST Brand & Assets](https://www.firstinspires.org/about/brand),
   the first pages of the
   [Branding & Design Guidelines](https://info.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/about/policy/first-brand-guidelines.pdf),
   [Youth Protection](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/youth-protection-program),
   and this playbook’s [Team Contract](../../../contract/) technology
   section. Write a one-page **consent and takedown** list: who can
   post, who can take a post down, what never gets posted (use the
   bullets above), and how photo consent is recorded. Put it in the
   team drive.

2. **Draft a two-week content calendar.** Six to ten rows. Each row
   has: date, channel, purpose (recruit, steward a sponsor, thank a
   host, explain a mechanism), owner, and the pitch or brand rule it
   must follow. At least one row is a stewardship update of the kind
   you wrote in
   [Sponsor Outreach & Stewardship](../sponsor-outreach/). At least
   one row is *not* a robot reveal.

3. **Write a five-line fact sheet.** Name, number, hometown or school,
   one-sentence pitch, season goal, team-owned contact, mentor
   contact. A reporter should be able to copy it without calling
   twice. No student last names unless the school already publishes
   them and a mentor said yes. No home addresses. No live travel
   plans.

4. **Review three recent posts.** Official channel or, if the team
   has none, three posts any teammate made about the team. Against
   the brand rules and the never-post list. Write a fix for each
   miss (or “clean” if it is clean). If a miss is still live and
   unsafe, tell the adult owner before you finish the ticket.

5. **Assign the two owners.** Student comms owner and adult takedown
   owner, written on the consent page. If either name is “whoever is
   free,” you do not have a standard.

6. **Hand the drive links to a mentor.** Calendar, fact sheet,
   consent/takedown page, post review. If your team exported these
   tickets, paste the links on the issue. Do not paste student last
   names or consent forms into a public tracker.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A consent and takedown page exists in the **team’s drive** and
      names who can post, who can take a post down, and what never
      gets posted.
- [ ] A two-week content calendar exists with owners and a purpose
      for each post, including one stewardship post.
- [ ] A five-line fact sheet exists that a mentor would let a
      reporter copy.
- [ ] Three recent posts were reviewed against brand and safety
      rules; misses have a written fix, and live unsafe posts were
      escalated.
- [ ] A student comms owner and an adult takedown owner are named.
- [ ] No consent form, student last name, or live travel plan was
      stored on this website.

## Notes

- A personal account that argues with another team is still the team
  in a stranger’s screenshot. Cheer. Do not negotiate.
- Sponsor logos are licensed by the promise you made, not by whatever
  looks good on a meme.
- Live-posting a hotel name or a departure time is a youth-protection
  problem, not a branding problem.
- The next ticket ([Year-Round Business Leadership](../year-round-leadership/))
  puts this calendar on a 12-month board so communications does not
  die in May.

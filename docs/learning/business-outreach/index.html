Business & Outreach Curriculum

A role-based path for the people who fund the team, tell its story, and grow its community. Work through the lessons in order: each one builds on the last, from a one-sentence pitch to an Impact submission a judge panel can trust.

Roles

Advocates grow through three roles. Core-path and Veteran are earned by completing the lessons labeled for each (and everything before it) and demonstrating the skills with real sponsors, events, or awards work. The Lead chair is an interview, not a ticket.

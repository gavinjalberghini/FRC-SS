## Overview

“We should do more outreach” is not a plan. An event is a plan: who it is
for, what they will do in 30 minutes, who on the team is running each
station, and how you will know it worked. This lesson is how to design one
that is worth the Saturday.

## Prerequisites

- [Team Story & Brand](../team-story-brand/)
- [Why Business Work Matters](../why-business-matters/)

## What you'll learn

- How to pick an event that matches a real goal.
- How to write a run-of-show and a staffing plan.
- How to follow up so the event is not a one-off photo.

## Unit 1 — Start from a goal

Pick one primary goal. Examples:

- Recruit members from a specific school or grade.
- Give a sponsor a visible, well-run hour.
- Teach a concrete skill to a rookie team or a classroom.
- Collect stories and photos for the Impact file (Lesson 9).

If the goal is “be visible,” rewrite it until you can count something.

## Unit 2 — Design the hour

- **Audience and count.** 15 third-graders is a different event than 4
  adult judges.
- **Stations.** One robot demo, one hands-on (drive a practice bot, build a
  simple mechanism), one story (students talking, not a mentor monologue).
- **Safety.** Glasses, battery rules, no unattended enabled robots. See
  [Electrical Safety]({{ '/learning/electrical/electrical-safety/' | relative_url }}).
- **Run-of-show** with clock times and named owners. “We’ll figure it out”
  is how you run late and look unserious.

## Unit 3 — Follow-up

Before you pack the trailer, assign:

- Who sends the thank-you the next day (host, sponsor, teachers).
- Who files photos, attendance, and a two-sentence “what we learned.”
- Who owns the next contact if someone asked how to join or help.

An event without a file is a memory. Memories do not win Impact and do not
recruit in August.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Write a one-page event brief: goal, audience, date, location, count.
- [ ] Write a run-of-show with times and student owners for each station.
- [ ] List PPE and robot-safe-state rules for that venue.
- [ ] Name the three follow-up tasks and who does them within 48 hours.

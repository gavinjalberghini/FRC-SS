## Overview

Sponsors are not ATMs. They are people buying a specific return: community
goodwill, student talent, a logo on a robot, or a story they can tell
internally. This lesson covers how to research a prospect and build a package
you can deliver.

## Prerequisites

- [Team Story & Brand](../team-story-brand/)
- [Season Budget](../season-budget/)

## What you'll learn

- The difference between a donation, a sponsorship, and a grant.
- How to research a company before you ask.
- How to build tiers you can actually fulfill.

## Unit 1 — Donation, sponsorship, grant

- A **donation** is a gift. Thank them; do not invent benefits you will forget.
- A **sponsorship** is a trade: money or in-kind goods for named benefits
  (logo, visit, report).
- A **grant** is a proposal against a funder’s written criteria, often with a
  report at the end.

Use the word that matches the relationship. Calling a $250 pizza gift a
“title sponsorship” trains people to ignore you.

## Unit 2 — Research before the ask

For each prospect, write down:

- What they already fund (education, manufacturing, local youth).
- Who the right person is (not “info@”).
- What you can honestly offer that matches their goals.
- A specific ask tied to a budget line (“travel to the district
  championship,” not “support robotics”).

Cold volume without research is spam. Ten researched asks beat a hundred
generic PDFs.

## Unit 3 — Packages you can deliver

A fair package is small and kept:

- **Logo on the robot / banner / website** — only if you will actually apply
  it and send a photo.
- **A shop visit or demo** — on a dated calendar, with a student host.
- **A mid-season and end-of-season update** — two emails, not a 20-page deck.

Never sell FIRST marks you cannot license. Never sell student time you cannot
staff. Under-promise.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Classify three past supporters as donation, sponsorship, or grant.
- [ ] Research one new prospect and write the four bullets in Unit 2.
- [ ] Draft a three-tier package and mark which benefits are already
      deliverable this season.
- [ ] Tie each tier to a budget line from the previous lesson.

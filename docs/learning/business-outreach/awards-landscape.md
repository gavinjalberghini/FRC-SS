## Overview

Awards are not a participation ribbon pile. Each one has a written purpose,
a submission path, and a judge behavior you can prepare for. This lesson is
the map so the team chases the right award on purpose — or none.

## Prerequisites

- [Why Business Work Matters](../why-business-matters/)

## What you'll learn

- The major judged awards and what they actually recognize.
- How judged awards differ from judged-on-the-spot pit conversations.
- When *not* to submit.

## Unit 1 — The awards that shape a season

Read the current year’s official descriptions. The names stay similar; the
criteria move. In most seasons you will care about:

- **Impact** (formerly Chairman’s) — sustained community impact, submitted
  in writing, then judged.
- **Engineering Inspiration** — the team that tells the story of FIRST
  exceptionally well, often with less paperwork and more presence.
- **Judges’ Award** — the thing judges could not ignore that does not fit
  another box.
- **Dean's List** — individual student leadership, nominated and
  interviewed.
- **Technical judged awards** (design, autonomous, imagery, etc.) — owned
  with the technical trades, not instead of them.

Do not treat Impact as “the business award” and ignore the rest. EI is often
won in the pit.

## Unit 2 — How judging actually works

- Judges are volunteers on a schedule. They have minutes, not hours.
- Written submissions are read before they walk up. The pit talk should
  match the paper.
- They compare teams in a district or regional, not against a fantasy
  national standard.
- Safety, student voice, and honesty beat a slick video that students
  cannot explain.

## Unit 3 — Choose on purpose

Submitting everything is not ambition; it is noise.

- Submit Impact only if the evidence file from Lesson 7 is real.
- Nominate Dean's List students who already lead, not students who “need a
  line on a resume.”
- Skip an award if nobody will own the interview.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Open this season’s official awards document and quote the Impact
      purpose in one sentence.
- [ ] Name which awards the team will pursue this year and who owns each.
- [ ] Explain how EI is different from Impact to someone on mechanical.
- [ ] List two things judges can verify in the pit in under two minutes.

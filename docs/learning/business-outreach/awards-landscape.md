## Description

Awards are not a participation ribbon pile. Each one has a written
purpose, a submission path, and a judge behavior you can prepare for.
This ticket is the map so the team chases the right award on purpose —
or none.

You now have an [evidence log](../measuring-impact/) in the team’s drive.
That file is raw material. It is not, by itself, a reason to submit
Impact. Submitting everything is not ambition; it is noise. Read this
season’s official pages. The names stay similar; the criteria move.

In most seasons you will care about:

- **FIRST Impact Award** (formerly Chairman’s). The most prestigious
  judged award in FRC. It honors the team that best represents a model
  for others to emulate and best embodies the mission of FIRST. It is
  a **written submission**, then an interview. Official home:
  [Impact Award Resources](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/fia-resources)
  and
  [Submitted Awards](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/submitted-awards).
  How it is judged: volunteers read the paper before they walk up;
  they look for claims they can verify; they compare you to the other
  teams at that event, not to a fantasy national standard. Student
  voice and honesty beat a slick video students cannot explain.
- **Engineering Inspiration Award.** Celebrates a team that advances
  respect for engineering in its school and community. Less paperwork
  than Impact, more **presence**. Official description:
  [Awards based on team attributes](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/team-awards).
  EI is often won in the pit. Do not treat Impact as “the business
  award” and ignore EI.
- **Judges’ Award.** The thing judges could not ignore that does not
  fit another box. You cannot really campaign for it. You can be
  worth noticing.
- **FIRST Leadership Award** (called the **Dean’s List Award** from
  2010–2025). Individual student leadership, nominated by mentors,
  then interviewed. Official guide:
  [2026 FIRST Leadership Award Guide (PDF)](https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/program/frc/awards/dla-guide.pdf)
  and the
  [Leadership Award page](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/first-leadership-award-winners).
  Nominate students who already lead, not students who “need a line
  on a resume.” Mentors still say “Dean’s List.” Both names are
  correct if you name the year.
- **Technical judged awards** (design, autonomous, imagery, and the
  rest). Owned **with** the technical trades, not instead of them.
  Business students help a designer tell the story. They do not
  write a mechanism they did not build.

How judging actually works, in practice:

- Judges are volunteers on a schedule. They have minutes, not hours.
  Read the
  [Judge Manual](https://info.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/program/frc/awards/judge-manual.pdf)
  far enough to respect that.
- Written submissions are read before they walk up. The pit talk
  should match the paper. A contradiction is a disqualifier in slow
  motion.
- Safety, student voice, and honesty beat production value.
- They can verify a number in the pit in under two minutes if you
  left them something to verify (a captioned photo, a partner letter,
  a budget snapshot). If you cannot show it, do not say it.

Choose on purpose:

- Submit Impact only if the evidence file from
  [Measuring Community Impact](../measuring-impact/) is real.
- Nominate Leadership Award students who already lead.
- Skip an award if nobody will own the interview.
- Read
  [2026 Season Award Updates](https://community.firstinspires.org/2025-season-award-updates-for-2026)
  so you are not working last year’s process.

The next ticket, [The Impact Award Submission](../impact-award/), is
multi-session writing. Do not start it because “we always submit.”
Start it because this ticket named an owner and a file that can
survive a judge.

This site does not track whether you submitted, and it does not host
essays. Keep the pursuit sheet in the team’s drive.

## Prerequisites

- [Why Business Work Matters](../why-business-matters/).
- [Measuring Community Impact](../measuring-impact/) completed — you
  know whether the evidence file is real enough to support Impact.
- A browser. You will open this season’s official award pages, not
  a teammate’s memory of 2022.

## What you'll learn

- The major judged awards and what they actually recognize.
- How judged awards differ from judged-on-the-spot pit conversations.
- How Impact is judged (paper first, then questions, then what they
  can verify).
- When *not* to submit.

## Tasks

1. **Open this season’s official awards pages.** Read the Impact
   paragraphs on
   [Submitted Awards](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/submitted-awards)
   and
   [Impact Award Resources](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/fia-resources).
   Quote the Impact **purpose** in one sentence, in your own words,
   then paste the official sentence under it so a mentor can see you
   did not invent a softer version.

2. **Separate EI from Impact.** Read the Engineering Inspiration
   description on
   [Awards based on team attributes](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/team-awards).
   Write four bullets a mechanical teammate could use: *Impact is …*,
   *EI is …*, *Impact is submitted how …*, *EI is won how …*. If you
   cannot tell them apart, you are not ready to pick.

3. **Name the individual award correctly.** Skim the
   [FIRST Leadership Award Guide](https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/program/frc/awards/dla-guide.pdf)
   (eligibility and who nominates). Write one sentence that uses
   **both** names: *The FIRST Leadership Award, formerly Dean’s List,
   is …* Add whether your team will nominate this year, and who
   would own the nomination — or “not this year, because …”.

4. **Write the pursuit sheet.** One page in the team drive:

   - which awards the team will pursue this year
   - who owns each (student + adult)
   - which awards you will **skip**, and why
   - two things judges can verify in the pit in under two minutes
     (a number from the evidence log, a partner letter, a labeled
     mechanism, a budget snapshot)

   Skipping is a decision. Leaving the line blank is not.

5. **Check the year’s updates.** Skim
   [2026 Season Award Updates](https://community.firstinspires.org/2025-season-award-updates-for-2026)
   (or this season’s equivalent if you are in a later year). Write
   one change that would affect your submission or interview plan.
   If nothing changed that you care about, write that — after you
   looked.

6. **Hand the sheet to a mentor.** If your team exported these
   tickets, paste the drive link on the issue. A mentor should be
   able to tell a drive-team lead which award you are actually
   chasing this weekend.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] You quoted this season’s official Impact purpose in one
      sentence and restated it in your own words.
- [ ] You can explain how Engineering Inspiration is different from
      Impact to someone on mechanical, using the four bullets.
- [ ] You can name the FIRST Leadership Award and its former name
      (Dean’s List), and say whether the team is nominating.
- [ ] A pursuit sheet in the **team’s drive** lists which awards you
      will pursue, who owns each, and which you will skip.
- [ ] The sheet lists two things judges can verify in the pit in
      under two minutes.
- [ ] A mentor can repeat the year’s Impact-or-not decision from the
      sheet without you in the room.

## Notes

- Criteria move. A teammate who “did Chairman’s in 2019” is a useful
  historian and a dangerous source of current rules. Open the page.
- You do not need to submit Impact to have a serious business team.
  You do need to decide.
- Do not nominate a student for the Leadership Award as a consolation
  prize. The interview will show it.
- The next ticket ([The Impact Award Submission](../impact-award/))
  is multi-session. Only start it if this sheet says you are
  submitting and the evidence file is real.

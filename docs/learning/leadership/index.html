Learning Hub · Leadership

Leadership Curriculum

A general path for any member growing into a leadership role — on a technical subsystem or a non-technical one. Work through the lessons in order: each one builds on the last, from leading yourself to leading a small team to developing the leaders who come after you. How the team staffs those roles — interviews, adult gap-fill, the four patterns we considered — lives on How We Staff Leadership, not in these tickets.

Roles

These lesson badges are a skill path, not the org-chart titles. Emerging Leader lines up with the core path, Team Leader with Veteran, Senior Leader with Lead. Finishing a badge does not give you the chair — how you move up is certification plus, for Lead, an interview.

Leadership is a skill, not a title. Anyone can practice these habits — you don't need to be a captain to lead a conversation, run a good meeting, or resolve a conflict well.

🚧

Leadership is practiced in the shop and at events, not only in these lessons. Use the acceptance criteria with a real group — a subsystem, a meeting, a conflict — so the habits stick.

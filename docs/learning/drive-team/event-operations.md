## Overview

Events are a logistics problem that happens to include matches. A crew that
can drive but cannot load in, queue, or share a station will still look
unready. This capstone is the weekend: from the trailer to the last cart
ride out.

## Prerequisites

- All prior Drive Team lessons.
- [Strategy: In-Event Iteration]({{ '/learning/strategy-scouting/in-event-iteration/' | relative_url }})

## What you'll learn

- How to load in and set a pit that can work.
- How to queue without panic.
- How the coach spends the time that is not a match.

## Unit 1 — Load-in and the pit

- A written load list (robot, batteries, bumpers, cart, spare modules,
  banner, safety glasses).
- Pit setup has a first hour: power, table, robot on blocks, battery
  shelf, tools. Decorations wait.
- Know the venue rules for batteries, power strips, and when you may
  enable.

## Unit 2 — Queue

- Leave the pit with time to *walk*, not jog. Late to queue is how you
  skip a checklist item.
- Cart path, bumper color, battery, auto — the
  [pre-match checklist](../pre-match-checklist/) happens here, not “on
  the field if we have time.”
- One person watches the queue clock. Everyone else does their job.

## Unit 3 — The station and the rest of the day

- Alliance-station etiquette: your alliance’s space, their auto, their
  coach. You are guests in a shared box.
- Between matches the coach is in debrief, then next-match plan, then
  rest. Not a roaming argument with strategy.
- Load-out is a reverse load-in. Lost tools are next week’s problem
  created today.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Write a load-in list and a first-hour pit plan.
- [ ] Time a full queue rehearsal from pit to “ready to push on.”
- [ ] Brief an alliance partner on your auto in under one minute.
- [ ] Run load-out with a checklist so the shop is missing nothing
      obvious.

## Description

Events are a logistics problem that happens to include matches. A crew
that can drive but cannot load in, queue, or share a station will still
look unready. This capstone is the weekend: from the trailer to the last
cart ride out.

You already have every artifact this weekend uses:

- role map and backups ([Drive Team Roles](../drive-team-roles/),
  [Practice Hours & Selection](../practice-selection/))
- DS fluency ([The Driver Station](../driver-station/))
- clock and rules card ([Match Flow & Field Rules](../match-flow-rules/))
- timed cycles ([Driving Fundamentals](../driving-fundamentals/))
- operator vocabulary ([Operator Skills](../operator-skills/))
- human-player signal ([Human Player](../human-player/))
- communication script ([In-Match Communication](../in-match-communication/))
- pre-match card ([Pre-Match Checklist](../pre-match-checklist/))
- 30-second pit report ([Pit Crew & Between-Match Repairs](../pit-and-repairs/))
- inspection punch-list ([Inspection & the Technician](../inspection-technician/))

This ticket is the motion that connects them: load-in, first hour in
the pit, queue without jogging, alliance-station etiquette, and
load-out. One voice to the driver still holds in someone else's
building. Mentors do not grab controllers on a real field any more
than they did in the shop. Practice hours on a queue rehearsal beat a
strong opinion about how "we'll be fine on Friday."

The
[Field Management System](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/technical-resources)
owns the field. You own being on time
([G301](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)),
being legal
([I102](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)),
and being decent guests in a shared alliance station.

This site does not track who loaded in or who started. Selection and
the weekend roster live with the team. If your team exported these
tickets, attach the load-in list and the queue-rehearsal time, then
close the issue once a mentor accepts the criteria below.

## Prerequisites

- All prior Drive Team tickets in this folder — or an honest GAP list
  for the ones you have not finished. Do not start an event on this
  ticket alone.
- [Strategy: In-Event Iteration](../../strategy-scouting/in-event-iteration/).
- Access to the cart, bumpers, batteries, and a space you can pretend
  is a pit and a queue.

## What you'll learn

- How to load in and set a pit that can work in the first hour.
- How to queue without panic, with the pre-match card done before you
  are on the carpet.
- How the coach spends the time that is not a match.
- How to leave with the same tools you brought.

## Tasks

1. **Read how the field will treat you.** Skim
   [FMS Whitepaper](https://fms-manual.readthedocs.io/en/latest/fms-whitepaper/fms-whitepaper.html)
   (what FMS Connected means — you already used this in
   [The Driver Station](../driver-station/)) and
   [Game Manual sections 5.12 and 6.3](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
   (Ethernet to the assigned station, operator console setup, drive-team
   staging). Then read
   [Operator Console Best Practices — At The Competition](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/stable/docs/software/driverstation/driver-station-best-practices.html).
   Write three venue rules on the load-in list: batteries, power
   strips, when you may enable in the pit. If the event page lists
   extras, add them. FIRST's
   [Season Materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials)
   and the event's own page beat a memory of last year.

2. **Write a load-in list and a first-hour pit plan.** One page,
   two columns:

   - **Load-in:** robot, batteries, bumpers both colors, cart, spare
     modules, the five spares from
     [Pit Crew](../pit-and-repairs/), banner, safety glasses,
     operator console, pre-match cards, communication script, role
     map, inspection punch-list and photos, scale if you have one.
   - **First hour:** power, table, robot on blocks, battery shelf,
     tools in homes. Decorations wait. Inspection walk starts when
     the robot can be on a cart, not when the banner is straight.

   Assign names or **GAP**. This website will not assign them.

3. **Time a full queue rehearsal.** From "pit, robot on the cart" to
   "ready to push on" using the real
   [pre-match card](../pre-match-checklist/): bumper color, battery,
   DS, USB, auto out loud, people, last-60 sentence. Walk, do not
   jog. One person watches a fake queue clock (a phone timer).
   Everyone else does their job. Write the time. If the card is still
   being run "on the field if we have time," the rehearsal failed.
   Do it again.

4. **Brief an alliance partner on your auto in under one minute.**
   Stand at a mock alliance station with two teammates playing
   another team. Coach says: auto path, where you will be in
   transition, endgame cue, one thing you need from them. Under 60
   seconds. No novel. You are guests in a shared box — their auto
   and their coach exist. Then switch: listen to their brief without
   talking over it. Watch
   [Einstein Final 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EjF9we707DA)
   if you need a picture of three stations that have to coexist.

5. **Run the between-match coach loop once.** After the queue
   rehearsal, pretend the match happened. Driver gives the
   30-second pit report. Coach does, in order: one breath, three-
   bullet debrief
   ([In-Match Communication](../in-match-communication/)), next-match
   one-sentence plan, rest. Not a roaming argument with strategy in
   the aisle. Not a mentor taking the sticks to "feel the drivetrain."
   If a repair is in progress, the coach updates the plan for a known
   limitation instead of hovering.

6. **Run load-out with a checklist.** Reverse the load-in list. Every
   line has a name and a check. Lost tools are next week's problem
   created today. A mentor walks the empty pit and the trailer (or
   the shop corner you used) and initials the card. The shop should
   be missing nothing obvious.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A load-in list and a first-hour pit plan exist with names or
      **GAP** on each line.
- [ ] A full queue rehearsal was timed from pit to "ready to push on"
      with the pre-match card completed *before* the fake field. A
      mentor watched.
- [ ] You briefed a mock alliance partner on auto in under one minute
      and then listened to theirs.
- [ ] The between-match coach loop (report → three bullets → next
      sentence → rest) was run once without a lecture in the aisle.
- [ ] Load-out was run against the list; a mentor initialed that the
      shop / trailer was not missing obvious tools.

## Notes

- Late to queue is how you skip a checklist item and then donate the
  match to a loose bumper. Leave the pit with time to walk.
- Never step onto the field until field staff signal. Never deploy
  code while FMS Connected.
- Alliance-station etiquette includes volume. Your script is for your
  driver. Their coach owns theirs.
- This is the last ticket in the drive-team sequence. The habits do
  not end here. Hours on a field, one voice, mentors off the sticks —
  those stay true in week six.

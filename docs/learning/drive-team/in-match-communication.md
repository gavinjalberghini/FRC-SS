## Overview

The alliance station is loud. Long sentences die. Panic is contagious. This
lesson is a small vocabulary, a coach who calls deviations, and the things
you do not say when the clock is red.

## Prerequisites

- [Drive Team Roles](../drive-team-roles/)
- [Operator Skills](../operator-skills/)
- [Strategy: Pre-Match Strategy]({{ '/learning/strategy-scouting/match-strategy/' | relative_url }})

## What you'll learn

- A default vocabulary for a match.
- How the coach uses the one-pager during teleop.
- What never belongs on the headset or in someone’s ear.

## Unit 1 — A small vocabulary

Agree on words before the event:

- **Clock** — “60,” “endgame,” “last cycle.”
- **Field** — “left,” “right,” “defend,” “clear.”
- **Robot** — “jam,” “comms,” “brownout,” “you’re clear.”
- **Plan** — “stick,” “switch,” “climb now.”

If a call needs a clause, it is too late or too early. Save it for the
debrief.

## Unit 2 — The coach’s job

- Before the match: confirm the one-pager. Auto, roles, endgame clock.
- During: watch the whole field. The driver cannot.
- Call **changes** (“you’re on defense,” “climb now”), not a stream of
  praise or blame.
- After: one breath, then the five-minute debrief. Not a lecture on the
  walk back.

## Unit 3 — Words you do not say

- Blame (“that’s on you”) during a live robot.
- Strategy arguments. The plan was closed in the pit.
- Mentors shouting over the glass. If they have a safety call, it is
  “disable” or “e-stop,” not a driving tip.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Publish a one-page vocabulary and use it in a full practice match.
- [ ] Coach a practice match using only deviation calls and the clock.
- [ ] List three phrases you will not use in the station.
- [ ] Debrief with the [in-event]({{ '/learning/strategy-scouting/in-event-iteration/' | relative_url }}) three-bullet format.

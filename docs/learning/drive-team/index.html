Learning Hub · Drive Team

Drive Team Curriculum

A role-based path for the people who operate the robot in a match and keep it running at an event. Work through the lessons in order: each one builds on the last, from knowing the roles to running the alliance station.

Roles

Crew members grow through three roles. Each role is earned by completing the lessons labeled for it (and everything before it) and demonstrating the skills in practice — on a field, in the pit, or in a real queue.

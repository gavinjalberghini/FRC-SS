## Description

You can drive well and still lose to a rule. Starting configuration, zone
lines, human-player timing, and contact rules are part of driving. This
ticket is the match clock and the field rules a crew member must know
without flipping the manual mid-queue.

You can already enable a robot from [The Driver Station](../driver-station/).
Enable is worthless if the robot is illegal when the field turns green.
[G303](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
is the start-your-robot rule: bumpers on the starting line, starting
configuration, inspected, not a hazard. [G301](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
is the be-prompt rule. Late to the station is how you skip the checklist
you will write in
[Pre-Match Checklist](../pre-match-checklist/).

In REBUILT a match is not a flat 150 seconds of the same game.
[Section 6.4](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
splits it:

- **AUTO** — 20 seconds. Hands off. The robot is a program.
  [G402](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
  is "let the ROBOT do its thing." Human players may still introduce
  fuel; drivers may not "help" the auto.
- A 3-second scoring delay, then **TELEOP** — 2:20.
- Teleop itself is a **TRANSITION SHIFT** (10 seconds, both hubs
  active), four **ALLIANCE SHIFTS** (25 seconds each, hubs alternate
  from the auto result), and **END GAME** (last 30 seconds, both hubs
  active, tower protection under
  [G420](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)).

The coach watches zones and contact so the driver can drive. One voice.
Mentors do not shout a rule interpretation through the glass during a
live robot. If you disagree with a call, the coach handles it after the
match through the
[Question Box (section 6.7.1)](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf).

This site does not track whether you memorized the times. If your team
exported these tickets, close this issue there once a mentor accepts the
criteria below.

## What you'll learn

- This season's match phases and the exact times, including when hubs
  are active.
- Common ways a legal robot becomes illegal on the field.
- How human-player and starting-position rules constrain the driver.
- Who talks to a referee, and when.

## Tasks

1. **Watch the official game, then the field.** Watch
   [REBUILT Presented by Haas — Game Animation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_fybREErgyM)
   once through. Then watch
   [2026 Additional Field Interactions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foDtQDAjteg)
   (FIRST Robotics Competition channel, about 2.5 minutes). Write the
   clock on an index card — this is the start of your pre-match card:

   - AUTO 0:20–0:00 (20 seconds)
   - TRANSITION 2:20–2:10
   - SHIFT 1–4, 25 seconds each (2:10 down to 0:30)
   - END GAME 0:30–0:00
   - Both hubs active in auto, transition, and endgame; one hub active
     per alliance shift, starting from the auto fuel result

   Recite the card out loud to a mentor. If you say "and then teleop"
   without the shifts, you are not done.

2. **Read the match-play rules that catch good drivers.** In the
   [2026 Game Manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
   (or the
   [HTML manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/HTML/2026GameManual.htm)),
   read section 6.3 (setup), 6.4 (periods), and these G-rules closely
   enough to explain each in one sentence:

   - **G303** — start configuration, bumpers, inspection.
   - **G402** — hands off in auto (except e-stop / A-stop; HP fuel is
     the documented exception).
   - **G407** — only score while your bumpers are in your alliance
     zone (launch rule — know this season's exact wording).
   - **G418** — 3-count on pins.
   - **G420** — do not contact an opponent on their tower in the last
     30 seconds.
   - **G421 / G423** — stay in your area; watch your reach.

   Write those six sentences on the back of the clock card. You will
   hand this card to the human-player ticket later.

3. **Sketch legal starting positions.** On a printed or hand-drawn field
   (the
   [Game Overview](https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/volunteer/frc/2026-rebuilt-game-overview.pdf)
   is enough geometry), mark:

   - your robot starting line and alliance zone
   - the outpost / human-player area
   - the tower and the last-30-seconds "do not wreck their climb" zone
   - red versus blue driver-station perspectives (mirrors matter)

   Stand on a shop floor taped to that sketch and say where the driver
   looks first in auto (nowhere — hands off) and first in teleop (the
   field, not the bumpers).

4. **Practice the auto hands-off.** On a practice robot or a mock
   station, run a 20-second "auto" with the DS in autonomous. Drivers
   take their hands off the sticks. The coach says nothing except
   "hands off" if someone twitches. If your team has no auto code,
   still run the 20 seconds of silence. The habit is the ticket, not
   the path planner.

5. **Role-play field staff.** One student is the driver, one is the
   coach, one is a referee who just called a pin. The driver wants to
   argue. The coach takes the question to a taped "question box" *after*
   the robot is disabled. Run it once the wrong way (driver yells) and
   once the right way (one breath, then the box). Read
   [section 6.7.1](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
   so you know the cap: up to two drive-team members, civil, student
   talking.

6. **Ask before you guess.** If any rule on your card is still a shrug,
   search
   [FRC Game Q&A](https://frc-qa.firstinspires.org/)
   for that rule number. Do not invent a house rule. Write the Q&A
   number on the card if you used one.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] You can recite this season's auto / transition / shift / endgame
      times and which hubs are active in each, without looking at a
      phone.
- [ ] A clock-and-rules card exists with the six G-rule sentences from
      Task 2. A mentor has heard you read it on a field or mock field.
- [ ] A field sketch shows legal starting positions and the human-player
      area for *your* robot.
- [ ] You demonstrated 20 seconds of hands-off auto at a mock station.
- [ ] You can explain who talks to a referee, when (after the match,
      Question Box), and who does not (the driver during a live robot).

## Notes

- Times move when FIRST publishes a Team Update. The commanding document
  is the English PDF on
  [Season Materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials),
  not this ticket and not a screenshot from kickoff week.
- Never step onto the field until field staff have turned the lights
  green (or the equivalent signal this season). "I was just grabbing
  the robot" is how people get hurt.
- FTA and referees are not opponents. Short, factual answers. The coach
  owns the conversation.
- The next ticket ([Driving Fundamentals](../driving-fundamentals/)) is
  structured practice. You now have a clock to practice against.

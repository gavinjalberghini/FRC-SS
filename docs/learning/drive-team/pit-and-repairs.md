## Overview

The pit is an emergency room with a schedule. The next match will arrive
whether the robot is ready or not. This lesson is triage: what to fix, what
to leave, and how to stop a helpful crowd from turning a 4-minute job into
a 20-minute one.

## Prerequisites

- [Pre-Match Checklist](../pre-match-checklist/)
- [Electrical: Systematic Troubleshooting]({{ '/learning/electrical/troubleshooting/' | relative_url }})

## What you'll learn

- How to take a 30-second report from the drive team.
- How to triage against the match clock.
- How to keep the pit safe and quiet enough to work.

## Unit 1 — The 30-second report

When the robot comes off the field, the driver or coach says:

- What failed (symptom, not theory): “intake dead,” “browned out twice,”
  “left rear module clicking.”
- When it started.
- Whether you can play the next match if that thing stays broken.

Then they get out of the way. Debugging-by-committee starts with six
people holding Allen keys.

## Unit 2 — Triage

- **Must fix to play** vs. **nice to fix** vs. **wait for a longer
  window**.
- Disconnect the battery before hands go in. Vent pneumatics. See
  [Electrical Safety]({{ '/learning/electrical/electrical-safety/' | relative_url }}).
- One technician leads. Others fetch, they do not invent a second
  diagnosis.
- If you cannot finish before queue, you go with a known limitation and
  a match-plan change — not a half-finished gearbox.

## Unit 3 — The pit itself

- Tools have homes. A floor full of parts is how you miss the match *and*
  a fastener.
- Spares for the things that actually break (belts, bumpers hardware,
  batteries, a module) beat a random bin.
- Food, homework, and visitors belong behind the tape, not on the robot.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Write the 30-second report script and use it after a practice
      “failure.”
- [ ] Time a known repair (battery, bumper, belt) and know if it fits
      between matches.
- [ ] Run a troubleshooting pass with one lead and a silent pit.
- [ ] List the five spares you will not go to an event without.

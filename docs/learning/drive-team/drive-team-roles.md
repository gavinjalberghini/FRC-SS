## Overview

A match is 150 seconds and several people. If two of them think they are
in charge of the same thing, you lose time; if nobody owns a thing, you
lose the match. This lesson names the roles so practice and events use the
same words.

## Prerequisites

- None. This is the starting point for every drive-team member.

## What you'll learn

- The five roles and their match jobs.
- Who speaks, who touches the robot, and who does not.
- How backups work.

## Unit 1 — The five seats

- **Driver** — translates the match plan into robot motion. Owns the
  drivetrain sticks. Does not argue strategy mid-match.
- **Operator** — owns mechanisms (intake, score, climb). Coordinates with
  the driver; does not steer.
- **Human player** — game pieces and station rules. Never reaches onto
  the field.
- **Technician** — the robot’s mechanical/electrical advocate in queue
  and between matches. Often the same person as a pit lead, not a sixth
  stick.
- **Drive coach** — the only person who talks strategy during the match.
  Holds the one-pager. Stands where the driver can hear them.

Some games combine driver and operator. The *jobs* still exist.

## Unit 2 — One robot, one voice

- The coach talks to the driver. The driver does not take advice from the
  stands, the human player, or a mentor behind the glass.
- Mentors do not grab a controller. If they do, the team is no longer
  student-driven in the only moment that is public.
- The technician does not “just tighten that” after the robot is staged
  unless the coach and FTA process allow it.

## Unit 3 — Backups

- Every seat has a named backup who has practiced that seat.
- Backups watch matches and run the checklist. They do not disappear
  until they are called.
- Switching seats mid-event is a coach decision, not a popularity vote
  after a bad match.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Name the five roles and one sentence for each job.
- [ ] State who is allowed to talk to the driver during a match.
- [ ] List the current starters and backups for each seat (or the gaps).
- [ ] Watch one match video and identify who is doing which job.

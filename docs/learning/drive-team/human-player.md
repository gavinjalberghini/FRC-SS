## Description

The human player is a match actor with a rulebook, not a spectator with
a better view. Illegal introductions, early steps, and "helpful" reaches
are free points for the other alliance. This ticket is the station job.

You already know the clock from
[Match Flow & Field Rules](../match-flow-rules/) and the three-word
operator vocabulary from [Operator Skills](../operator-skills/). The
human player does not add a fourth voice. Eye contact or one hand
signal. The coach can relay "we need pieces now" or "stop, we're
defending." Never coach the driver from the outpost. That is a second
strategy channel, and second channels lose matches.

In REBUILT the human player works an
[OUTPOST](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
(section 5.9.2): a chute of fuel, a door you rotate, a corral where
robots can return fuel, and tape lines that mark where you may not
reach. [G421](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
keeps you in your area.
[G423](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
is "watch your reach."
[G402](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
lets you introduce fuel in auto; it does not let you reach onto the
field. [G405](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
is keep scoring elements on the field (the outpost opening is the
documented path). If you need a teammate to shout the rule, you are not
ready.

Mentors do not step into the outpost "to show you how to throw." They
can stand behind the tape and watch your feet. Practice hours at a
mock chute beat a strong opinion about your arm.

This site does not track who is starting at human player. If your team
exported these tickets, close this issue there once a mentor accepts the
criteria below.

## Prerequisites

- [Match Flow & Field Rules](../match-flow-rules/) — clock card and the
  six G-rule sentences.
- [Drive Team Roles](../drive-team-roles/) — you know you are not a
  second coach.
- This season's human-player / outpost rules in the
  [2026 Game Manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
  (sections 5.9.2, 6.3.1, G421, G423, G402, G405).

## What you'll learn

- This season's human-player rules at a usable level: where you stand,
  what you may touch, when a piece is live.
- How to time introductions to the robot's cycle instead of the other
  way around.
- One signal for "no more pieces," and why you never add a speech.

## Tasks

1. **Watch the official outpost, then a match.** Watch
   [2026 Additional Field Interactions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=foDtQDAjteg)
   and re-watch the human-player moments in
   [REBUILT Game Animation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_fybREErgyM).
   Write three things the animation shows a human player doing and one
   thing it does *not* show (reaching through the alliance wall, walking
   onto carpet, coaching). Keep the list.

2. **Quote the three rules that matter most this season.** Open the
   [2026 Game Manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/2026GameManual.pdf)
   (or the
   [HTML manual](https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/HTML/2026GameManual.htm))
   and write, in your own words, not a screenshot:

   - Where you may stand, and when you may enter or leave the outpost
     area (G421 and section 6.3.1).
   - What you may touch, and what you may not (chute door, fuel,
     nothing on the field side of the tape — G423).
   - When a piece is live, and what happens if you drop or send it
     illegally (G405 and any Team Update that changed it).

   If Q&A changed a detail, add the
   [FRC Game Q&A](https://frc-qa.firstinspires.org/)
   number. These three sentences go on the back of the clock card.

3. **Build a mock outpost and walk it.** Tape an outpost area on the
   shop floor: a "chute," a door you can open and close (cardboard is
   fine), a tape line you may not reach past, and a corral. Stand in
   the box. Have a mentor or coach try to bait you into stepping over
   the line to "help." Stay in the box. Say the three rules out loud.

4. **Run a 10-cycle feed drill.** Pair with the driver/operator from
   [Operator Skills](../operator-skills/). They run the cycle drill.
   You introduce a piece (or a stand-in) only when the intake is
   actually ready — watch *your* robot, not the whole field. If the
   robot is late, wait. Forcing a piece into a closed intake is how
   you cause a jam the pit cannot clear in one cycle. A third person
   times whether each piece arrived on time or early/late. Ten cycles.
   Log them next to the driver's times.

5. **Demonstrate a legal miss recovery.** Drop a piece on your side of
   the tape, or miss the chute on purpose. Recover without crossing
   onto the field and without throwing in a way the manual forbids.
   If you cannot do this safely with real fuel, do it with the stand-in
   and *say* the illegal alternative you are not choosing.

6. **Agree on one signal for "no more pieces."** With the driver and
   coach, pick one hand signal or eye-contact rule. Write it on the
   role map. The coach may relay it; you do not shout across the
   field. Practice it once during the feed drill. That signal becomes
   part of the communication script in
   [In-Match Communication](../in-match-communication/).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] You can quote this season's three most important human-player
      rules from memory (stand/reach, touch, live piece). A mentor
      heard you on a mock outpost, not only in a classroom.
- [ ] A 10-cycle feed drill is logged next to the driver's cycle times.
      Pieces arrived on the robot's schedule, not yours.
- [ ] You demonstrated a legal drop / miss recovery without entering
      the field.
- [ ] One "no more pieces" signal is written on the role map and was
      used in the drill.
- [ ] You can state that the human player never coaches the driver.

## Notes

- FIRST allows up to three students as drivers or human players
  combined. If an alliance is short, a student technician may become a
  human player for that match only — Head Referee notified, all HP
  rules apply, they are no longer the technician for that match. Read
  the paragraph under Table 6-1 before you invent a workaround.
- Stools exist at events for sightlines. Ask the FTA before matches if
  you need one. Do not stand on a cart.
- The next shared ticket for the whole crew is
  [In-Match Communication](../in-match-communication/). Bring the
  three-word operator list and this one signal with you.

## Overview

The human player is a match actor with a rulebook, not a spectator with a
better view. Illegal introductions, early steps, and “helpful” reaches are
free points for the other alliance. This lesson is the station job.

## Prerequisites

- [Match Flow & Field Rules](../match-flow-rules/)

## What you'll learn

- This season’s human-player rules at a usable level.
- How to time introductions to the robot’s cycle.
- How to stay useful when the plan changes.

## Unit 1 — The rules are the job

Read the current human-player section until you can answer:

- Where you may stand, and when you may enter or leave the station.
- What you may touch, and what you may not.
- When a piece is live, and what happens if you drop or throw illegally.

If you need a teammate to shout the rule, you are not ready.

## Unit 2 — Timing the robot

- Watch *your* robot’s intake, not the whole field.
- Have pieces staged the way the driver expects (orientation, count).
- If the robot is late, wait. Forcing a piece into a closed intake is how
  you cause a jam the pit cannot clear in one cycle.

## Unit 3 — Communication

- Eye contact with the driver or a single hand signal. Not a speech.
- The coach can relay “we need pieces now” or “stop, we’re defending.”
- Never coach the driver from the HP station. That is a second voice.

## Steps & acceptance criteria

- [ ] Quote this season’s three most important HP rules from memory.
- [ ] Run a 10-cycle practice feeding a real or mock intake on time.
- [ ] Demonstrate a legal drop / miss recovery without entering the
      field.
- [ ] Agree on one signal with the driver for “no more pieces.”

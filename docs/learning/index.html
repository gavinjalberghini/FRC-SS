Grow into your role

Learning Hub

Curated, role-based tickets to help every member build skills — whether you're turning a wrench, writing robot code, or pitching sponsors. Pick the trade you are actually on (see the org chart). Tickets teach and certify; they do not appoint you Lead. This site does not track student work.

Tracks

Each track is a sequence of tickets: a lesson that teaches, explicit tasks, and acceptance criteria a mentor can sign. Pick the track that matches your role and work through it in order. Teams that want a board should export the tickets into their own GitHub.

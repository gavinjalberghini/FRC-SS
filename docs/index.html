The FRC Playbook

An opinionated take on how to run a great robotics team.

A comprehensive resource containing deliberated opinions that organizes people, sets expectations, plans the build season, and helps every member grow into their role.

Start here

The playbook is organized around the few things every member should understand: who we are, who does what, how we staff leaders, how we decide, the core principles of each trade, how we behave, how the season runs, and where to learn.

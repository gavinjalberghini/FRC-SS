## Mission Statement

> Our mission is to build excitement and engagement around STEM throughout our
> community by giving students the opportunity to take on tough, real-world
> engineering challenges.
>
> We design those challenges to push students to learn new skills, work
> cooperatively as a team, and make a positive impact — both in their own lives
> and in the community around them. Our teams are **student-driven**: we build
> organizational patterns that elevate capable students so they can shine, while
> growing and supporting everyone who wants to step into a leadership role.
>
> In everything we set out to accomplish, we live the values of **Coopertition®**
> and **Gracious Professionalism®**, and we treat failure not as defeat but as a
> natural, essential part of learning. We welcome students of every background and
> skill level, because we believe these experiences build not just better
> engineers, but more confident, capable, and community-minded people — long after
> the final match.

## Vision

We want to be a team that performs well not once, but consistently — and we
believe consistency is built by people, not trophies. Our vision rests on three
commitments:

### Consistent performance through mentorship and shared learning

We want to compete consistently and qualify for championship-level events. The
way we get there is by instilling **mentorship and shared learning** in every
student. We aren't trying to create one-time all-stars; we're developing
individuals who can impart their knowledge and impact to the next generation.
That takes **foresight** and a **commitment to each other** — investing in
teammates today so the team is stronger long after any one of us has moved on.

- Build a culture where students teach students and knowledge is passed down, not
  lost to graduation.
- Develop depth at every role so the team never depends on a single person.
- Plan ahead — for the next match, the next season, and the next generation.

### An increased impact on our community

We want to grow our impact beyond our own team. The goal is to **share our
opportunities and excitement with others** so that the whole STEM community
around us is elevated.

- Mentor and support rookie and nearby teams.
- Share designs, code, and lessons learned openly.
- Bring the excitement of STEM to our schools and broader community.

### A student-led team

We want to remain **student-led** in the sense this playbook uses everywhere
else: **student-driven, mentor-supported**. Mentors coach rather than hover.
Students take genuine **ownership and responsibility** for real-world
challenges — the chairs they are ready to hold — and grow into leaders
through that responsibility. Adults fill empty chairs and hold veto. They
do not run every meeting.

- Put students in charge of real decisions and subsystems — the chairs they
  are ready to hold. Spend inside the season plan is a student-shaped call;
  **unbudgeted** money is an adult call (see
  [Decision-Making]({{ '/decision-making/' | relative_url }})).
- Use mentors as coaches and guides, not as a shadow cabinet: they occupy
  empty chairs until a student can take them, and they hold veto. That
  synthesis is in
  [How We Staff Leadership]({{ '/staffing-leadership/' | relative_url }}).
- Sustain a strong pipeline of new members and emerging leaders year over year.

## Core Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Gracious Professionalism®** | We compete fiercely and treat everyone — teammates and opponents — with respect. |
| **Safety first** | No deadline is worth an injury. We follow shop rules, every time. |
| **Learn by doing** | Members own real work. Mistakes are expected and are how we learn. |
| **Everyone contributes** | Technical and non-technical roles are equally valued. |
| **Leave it better** | We document, clean up, and hand off a stronger team to next year. |
